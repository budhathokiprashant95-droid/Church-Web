Our Story & Vision

Grace Church began with a small, faithful gathering in Lalbandi, Sarlahi, with a mission to share the grace and love of Jesus Christ with our community. Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant family of believers, dedicated to worship, discipleship, and outreach.

We envision a community where everyone feels welcomed, supported, and inspired to live out their faith every day.

What We Believe

Biblical Truth: Study and follow God's word.

Salvation: Finding hope, faith, and grace in Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To share the love of Jesus Christ, build a strong community of faith, and serve people with compassion and humility.
