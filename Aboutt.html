Our Story & Vision

Lalbandi Friends Church began with a small, faithful gathering in Lalbandi, Sarlahi, Nepal, with a mission to share the grace and love of Jesus Christ with our community. Over the years, we have grown into a family of believers, dedicated to worship, discipleship, and outreach.

We envision a community where everyone feels welcomed, supported, and inspired to live out their faith every day.

What We Believe
